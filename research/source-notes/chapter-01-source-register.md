## S01 — UNDP Human Development Report 2025

- **Source ID:** S01
- **Author or institution:** United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Human Development Report Office (HDRO)
- **Year:** 2025
- **Exact title:** Human Development Report 2025: A Matter of Choice — People and Possibilities in the Age of AI
- **Source type:** Multilateral institutional report (flagship publication)
- **Country or region:** Global, with country-specific data for 193 countries and territories
- **Official URL:** https://hdr.undp.org/reports/global/human-development-report-2025
- **Access date:** 2025-07-30
- **Relevant sections or pages:** Statistical Annex Table 1 (HDI and its components, 2023 data); Table 3 (Inequality-adjusted HDI); country profiles for India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Afghanistan
- **Key claims supported:** India HDI value 0.685 (2023), rank 130/193; South Asia regional HDI 0.672 (2023); IHDI for India 0.475 with 30.7% loss due to inequality; life expectancy, education, and GNI per capita data for all South Asian countries; HDI category thresholds
- **Reliability assessment:** Highest — UNDP flagship report, peer-reviewed methodology, official UN data source
- **Limitations:** Data for 2023 published in 2025; some component data may be estimated for countries with limited statistical capacity
- **Used in Chapter 1:** yes
