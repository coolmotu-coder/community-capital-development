- **Author or institution:** Christiaan Grootaert and Nicolas van Bastelaer
- **Publication year:** 2000
- **Exact title:** Measuring social capital: An integrated questionnaire (World Bank Working Paper No. 18)
- **Source type:** Multilateral institutional working paper
- **Country or region:** Global (cross-country survey instrument)
- **Official or canonical URL:** https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/515261468740392133/pdf/281100PAPER0Measuring0social0capital.pdf
- **Access date:** 2025-07-30
- **Relevant pages, tables, sections, or dataset fields:** Structural social capital module (group memberships, organizational participation); cognitive social capital module (trust, reciprocity, cooperation); questionnaire design for social capital measurement
- **Exact claims supported in this Volume III section:** CHCS Section E (Social Capital) adapted from SCAT structural and cognitive dimensions; trust and reciprocity 1-5 scale questions; organizational membership enumeration; collective action measurement
