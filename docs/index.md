## Three-Volume Structure

The project is organized into three complementary volumes, each serving a distinct function within the overarching research program:

### Volume I — Theoretical Foundations and Conceptual Frameworks

Volume I is the starting volume of the publication. It opens with Chapter 1 and proceeds through Chapter 15, establishing the theoretical underpinnings, defining each form of capital examined by the project, and providing the methodological grounding for household capital analysis and class-based development research.

> **Status:** _In Progress_ — Chapters 1–15 are available. Chapter 1 is the first working chapter; Chapters 2–15 are structured placeholders.

### Volume II — Household Capital Class Structures and Comparative Analysis

Volume II constitutes the empirical core of the project. It systematically classifies household types based on their capital endowments, presents comparative analyses across class categories, and develops the Household Capital Development Index. It contains Chapters 16–25.

> **Status:** _In Progress_ — Chapters 16–25 are available. Content continues to be developed, reviewed, and expanded.

### Volume III — Assessment Tools, Questionnaires and Implementation Guides

Volume III translates the theoretical frameworks of Volume I and the classifications of Volume II into practical, field-ready instruments.

> **Status:** _Proposed_ — Structural outline in development.
