# Volume III: Assessment Tools, Questionnaires and Implementation Guides

## Overview

Volume III translates the theoretical frameworks of Volume I and the classifications of Volume II into practical, field-ready instruments. It provides assessment tools, standardized questionnaires, scoring methodologies, implementation guides, and illustrative case studies for development practitioners and policymakers.

## Status

> **Status:** _Proposed_ — Structural outline in development. Individual sections are under development.

## Contents

### Assessment Tools

Standardised instruments for measuring household capital endowments across multiple dimensions.

### Questionnaires

Structured data collection instruments for community-level and household-level surveys.

### Scoring Methodology

Guidelines and procedures for scoring household capital profiles and computing composite indices.

### Implementation Guides

Step-by-step instructions for deploying assessment tools in field settings.

### Case Studies

Illustrative examples demonstrating the application of assessment tools across different community contexts.
