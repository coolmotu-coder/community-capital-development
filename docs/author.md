# Author

## Anil Kumar Srivastava

Anil Kumar Srivastava is the author and principal researcher of the Community Capital Development Project.

## Research Interests

The author's research spans:

- Household capital formations and class structures
- Development economics and political economy
- Mixed-methods field research design
- Participatory development frameworks
- Community-level assessment and monitoring
- Policy analysis for inclusive development

## Author's Note

This project represents years of research, fieldwork, and theoretical development. The frameworks and classifications presented are original contributions to the literature on household capital and class analysis. I welcome constructive feedback, corrections, and suggestions for improvement from readers, researchers, and development practitioners.
