# Volume I: Theoretical Foundations and Conceptual Frameworks

## Overview

Volume I establishes the theoretical underpinning for the entire Community Capital Development Project. It introduces core concepts, defines each form of capital examined by the project, and provides the methodological grounding for household capital analysis and class-based development research.

Volume I is the first volume of the publication and opens with Chapter 1, which sets out the background, rationale, and structure of the entire project.

## Scope

This volume covers:

- The meaning of community development and its relationship to capital
- The concept of capital in development studies and interdisciplinary contexts
- Multiple specific forms of capital: social, human, physical, natural, financial, cultural, political, intellectual, digital, and psychological
- An integrated multi-capital analytical framework
- A proposed Community Capital Development Index
- Research design, indicator framework, and implementation guidance
