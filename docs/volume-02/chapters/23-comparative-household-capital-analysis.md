> **`[Draft]`** This chapter is under active development. Comparative data summaries and statistical analysis will be incorporated as research progresses.

## 23.1 Introduction

This chapter presents a comparative analysis of household capital endowments across the class categories established in the preceding chapters (Chapters 16–22). The analysis aggregates across capital dimensions to illuminate patterns of inequality, convergence, and divergence between household categories.

## 23.2 Analytical Approach

The comparative analysis employs multi-dimensional visualization and aggregation techniques to:

- Display household capital profiles across categories
- Identify dimension-specific patterns of advantage and disadvantage
- Highlight inter-category similarities and distinctions
- Examine the relationship between capital configuration and developmental outcomes
