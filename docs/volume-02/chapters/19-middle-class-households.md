## 19.1 Introduction

Middle-Class Households represent a category with substantive capital endowments across most dimensions. These households typically achieve a degree of economic security and social stability, though their capacity for significant capital expansion and intergenerationaladvancement varies considerably.

## 19.2 Defining Characteristics

Middle-Class Households are characterized by:

- Stable and diversified financial capital with regular savings behavior
- Substantial physical assets including quality housing and productive equipment
- High educational attainment with specialized skills
- Broad and diverse social networks with multi-directional flows of support
- Active and strategic engagement with formal institutions

## 19.4 Capital Maintenance and Expansion

A distinguishing feature of Middle-Class Households is the significance of capital preservation alongside accumulation. These households typically develop strategies for protecting existing assets against inflation, economic downturns, and social risks while pursuing measured expansion.

## 19.5 Intergenerational Transmission

Intergenerational transmission of capital is a central concern for Middle-Class Households. Educational investment, customary savings for children, and networks of support across kin and community are key mechanisms for maintaining class position.
