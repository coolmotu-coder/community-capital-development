# Publication Roadmap

This page outlines the planned publication timeline and milestones for the Community Capital Development Project.

## Current Status

| Volume | Title | Status | Chapters |
|--------|-------|--------|----------|
| Volume I | Theoretical Foundations and Conceptual Frameworks | In Progress | 15 chapters — Chapter 1 is full working content; 2–15 are structured placeholders |
| Volume II | Household Capital Class Structures and Comparative Analysis | In Progress | 10 chapters (16–25) |
| Volume III | Assessment Tools, Questionnaires and Implementation Guides | Proposed | Structural outline in development |

## Milestones

### Completed

- Repository creation and site infrastructure setup
- MkDocs Material site generator configuration
- GitHub Actions automated deployment pipeline
- Volume II chapters 16–25 initial drafts
- Volume I Chapter 1 full working content
- Volume I Chapters 2–15 structured placeholders

### In Progress

- Full development of Volume I chapters 2–15
- Full development of Volume II chapters with empirical data and analysis
- Peer review and community feedback integration
- Volume III assessment tool construction

### Planned

- Completion of Volume I theoretical foundations
- Completion of Volume II empirical analysis
- Volume III publication with field-ready assessment instruments
- Cross-volume integration and cross-referencing
- Translation and localization considerations
- Conference presentations and academic dissemination
