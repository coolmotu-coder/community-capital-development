# Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used throughout the Community Capital Development Project.

## A

**Asset** — A resource-owned by an individual, household, or community—that provides economic value, social standing, or developmental capacity.

## C

**Capital (in this project)** — A broad category encompassing economic, social, cultural, and institutional endowments that households accumulate and deploy to improve their welfare and developmental trajectories.

**Capital Transformation** — The process by which households convert one form of capital into another (e.g., social capital into economic capital).

**Class Analysis** — The systematic examination of social stratification based on differential access to and control over capital resources.

**Community Capital** — The aggregate of capital resources available within a community, including both household-level and shared-institutional endowments.

## D

**Development Index** — A composite measure designed to capture multiple dimensions of developmental well-being beyond income or wealth alone.

## E

**Endowment** — The initial stock of capital resources possessed by a household or individual at a given point in time.

## H

**Household Capital** — The total capital endowment of a household, comprising economic, social, cultural, and institutional dimensions.

**Household Capital Development Index** — An original framework that aggregates financial, physical, human, social, and institutional capital into a composite index for comparative household classification.

## I

**Institutional Capital** — The resources derived from relationships with formal and informal institutions, including access to services, legal protections, and policy influence.

## L

**Livelihood Strategy** — The spectrum of activities, choices, and means that households employ for securing their basic necessities and developmental progress.

## M

**Mixed-Methods Research** — A research approach that integrates qualitative and quantitative methods to provide a more comprehensive understanding of a phenomenon.

## P

**Policy Framework** — A structured set of principles, guidelines, and recommendations designed to inform decision-making and program implementation.

## S

**Stratification** — The hierarchical ordering of social groups based on differential access to resources, power, and prestige.
